THE IDEA

The useful part
is the graph.

Before implementation, someone has to decide what the pieces are, how they fit together, and what each piece must prove.

Srashta gives those decisions a durable home. Your planning agent authors the graph. The CLI checks its structure, derives execution waves and packages focused briefs.

A better starting plan. Less correction along the way.

ACCEPTANCE
  • A note has an integer ID, title and timestamps.
  • The title is required and limited to 120 characters.
  • The approved contract defines create, read and error responses.

cd srashta

source .venv/bin/activate
python -m pip install -e .
